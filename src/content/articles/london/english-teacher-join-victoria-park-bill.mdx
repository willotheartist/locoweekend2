The confirmed draw, for now, is [English Teacher](/articles/yard-act-take-november-english-teacher-takes-the-support-slot) — the Mercury-winning Leeds band added as a late addition to the bill, which is either a coup or a placeholder depending on how generously you're feeling.

A day ticket is £62.50, on sale Thursday 12 June. That's the concrete part. The rest — who tops the bill, how many acts come in the second wave, what the running order looks like — remains firmly in the hands of the organisers and their July reveal. You are, in effect, purchasing partial information and a date in your diary.

## The One Thing That's Certain

English Teacher are the only named act, which makes them do a lot of heavy lifting for a band billed as a late addition rather than a headliner. Whether that's the right amount of weight for £62.50 and a blank space where the top of the bill should be is a calculation only you can run. The on-sale is Thursday 12 June. Make your peace with the unknowns before then, or don't — but don't say you weren't told.

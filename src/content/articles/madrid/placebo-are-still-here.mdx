There are bands you grow out of and bands you grow back into. Placebo have spent thirty years being both simultaneously, which is either their greatest trick or the reason you're already rolling your eyes. Either way, they've announced a worldwide 30th Anniversary Tour under the concept Re:Created, and Spain is on the itinerary for October 2026 — Madrid on the 1st, Barcelona on the 3rd.

## The Concept, For What It's Worth

Re:Created is the name hanging over the whole tour, which suggests the band have something more structured in mind than a greatest-hits lap of honour. Whether that means reworked arrangements, visual theatre, or something genuinely difficult to sit through is not yet known, and we're not going to speculate. What we know is that it's a worldwide run, Spain is part of it, and they've chosen to frame thirty years as a starting point rather than a full stop. That's either admirable or exhausting depending on your history with them.

## Madrid Gets the Arena

The Madrid show lands at the Movistar Arena on 1 October 2026, doors — or rather, start time — listed at 18:45, which is aggressively early by this city's standards. You will need to have eaten before you go, or accept that you're eating after midnight, which is hardly a punishment. Tickets are available through Ticketmaster, El Corte Inglés and Entradas.com, so the usual obstacle course applies. Barcelona follows two nights later on the 3rd.

If you were sixteen in the late nineties and owned a particular kind of eyeliner, you already know whether you're going. Everyone else should probably go anyway — thirty years is a long time to still be this divisive, and that alone earns a look.

Madrid’s nightlife is famous for its energy, but the city has quietly developed one of Europe’s most interesting cocktail scenes.

Bars here combine theatrical presentation with serious technical skill.

## Salmon Guru

**[Salmon Guru](https://salmonguru.es/)** regularly appears on the World's 50 Best Bars list.

Its psychedelic interior and elaborate cocktails make it one of the most distinctive bars in Spain.

## Del Diego

**[Del Diego](https://bardeldiego.com/)** represents Madrid’s classic cocktail tradition.

Operating since 1991, it remains one of the city’s most respected bars, known for flawless execution and timeless drinks.

## 1862 Dry Bar

**[1862 Dry Bar](https://1862drybar.com/)** helped introduce craft cocktail culture to Madrid.

It specialises in classic recipes executed with precision.

Madrid’s cocktail culture reflects the city itself: energetic, slightly theatrical, and designed for long nights.

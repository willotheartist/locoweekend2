<h2 id="dark">Dark: the mystery that asks you to pay attention</h2>

**Format:** three seasons. **Original language:** German. **Genre:** science fiction / mystery. Netflix’s UK listing describes a missing child and a mystery connecting four families across generations. [Official title information and trailer](https://www.netflix.com/gb/title/80100172).

The appeal lies in the relationship between its puzzle and its people. Family connections are not decorative background; they are the machinery of the mystery. That makes it a strong choice when you want to spend an evening following details rather than simply absorbing atmosphere. It is a poor candidate for watching while answering messages.

<h2 id="the-queens-gambit">The Queen’s Gambit: a finite commitment with an expansive world</h2>

**Format:** limited series. **Original language:** English. **Genre:** drama. Anya Taylor-Joy stars as a chess prodigy whose rise is complicated by addiction. Netflix credits Scott Frank and Allan Scott as creators. [Official title information and episodes](https://www.netflix.com/gb/title/80234304).

Chess provides the structure, but you do not need an opening repertoire to understand the stakes. Competition gives the story deadlines and adversaries; the more interesting question is what exceptional ability does to a person who has difficulty finding stability elsewhere. It is the natural starting point here for someone who wants one contained story rather than several seasons of commitment.

<h2 id="stranger-things">Stranger Things: begin with the small-town story</h2>

**Format:** Netflix’s UK page lists five seasons; this recommendation rates **season one only**. **Original language:** English. **Genre:** science fiction / suspense. The premise joins a boy’s disappearance, secret experiments and a girl with unusual abilities. [Official title information](https://www.netflix.com/gb/title/80057281).

The first season’s appeal is its manageable scale: a group of friends, adults trying to understand what is happening, and a town whose ordinary surfaces conceal something frightening. Nostalgia gives it a familiar vocabulary, but friendship gives the adventure a reason to matter. Start there instead of treating the size of the franchise as an obligation.

<h2 id="altered-carbon">Altered Carbon: choose the first season for the world</h2>

**Format:** Netflix’s UK page lists two seasons; this score covers **season one only**. **Original language:** English. **Genre:** science fiction / crime. Its premise follows a prisoner returned to life in another body and tasked with solving a murder. [Official title information](https://www.netflix.com/gb/title/80097140).

The interesting tension is between the detective plot and the world it opens up. When bodies become replaceable, wealth and identity acquire a particularly unpleasant relationship. That premise gives the familiar investigation a larger subject: who gets to treat mortality as a problem that money can solve? Choose it when elaborate world-building is part of the entertainment, not an obstacle to it.

## How to choose between them

Choose **Dark** when you want concentration to be rewarded. Choose **The Queen’s Gambit** when the attraction is a contained dramatic arc. **Stranger Things season one** is the most direct route here into an adventure built around friendship. **Love, Death & Robots** offers the freedom of short, separate stories. **Altered Carbon season one** is the pick when the imagined world is as important to you as the plot.

Those differences matter more than pretending that a five-point score can predict everybody’s taste. A lower-scoring recommendation can still be the better choice for your particular evening. Read the caveat before you press play.

## What does a LocoWeekend rating mean?

**5/5 means exceptional; 4/5 means a strong recommendation; 3/5 means worth a look; 2/5 means for committed fans; 1/5 means skip.** We consider story, craft, distinctiveness and the reward for the time involved. The score is a judgement, not a mathematical average of those qualities.

This is a recommendations list, so it naturally contains strong scores. That does not mean every title we cover will earn four or five. Our [full rating guide](/movies-series/how-we-rate) explains the scale and how we distinguish a season rating from a whole-series assessment.
